How to manage your Paradox Sort data.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Paradox Sort is provided by Pete’s Paradox GAMES. You can play as a guest or choose to sign in where player accounts are available.

Delete your Paradox Sort account

In the game, open Gallery → Profile → Delete account. Confirm with the same sign-in account you used for Paradox Sort. This deletes your Paradox Sort account and synced level progress. The copy of your progress on this device remains available as a guest save. You can clear that copy separately using the steps below.

Delete game data on your device

You can remove locally stored Paradox Sort data using your Android device’s app settings: open Settings → Apps → Paradox Sort → Storage and choose the option to clear app data or storage. Menu names vary by device. On iOS, delete Paradox Sort from your device to remove its locally stored app data; choosing “Offload App” keeps documents and data. Clearing storage deletes the local game data, including progress, in-game rewards, cosmetics and settings; this can’t be undone. Device or platform backups may retain or restore copies depending on your settings.

Request help or deletion without reinstalling

You can contact us without reinstalling the game. Email pete@petesparadox.games with the subject Paradox Sort data deletion request. To request deletion of your Paradox Sort account and cloud save, tell us which sign-in provider you used. If you know the email address associated with the Paradox Sort account, include it so we can locate the account. Please don’t send passwords, one-time codes or payment-card details. We may ask for information needed to verify a request.

Once we verify the request, we can process deletion of the Paradox Sort account and its synced progress. Device-only data can be cleared using the steps above. Use Google or Apple’s own controls to manage the separate account you use to sign in.

Google Play Games data

Google Play Games is a separate Google service. To manage or delete data in your Google Play Games profile, follow Google’s instructions for deleting Play Games data. Deleting Google Play Games data does not automatically remove information held by the game developer, and clearing local game data does not necessarily delete data held by Google.

Retention

Synced level progress is kept while you use the account and removed when the account is deleted. We retain a deletion record linked to the former player ID to prevent an older session from restoring the deleted cloud save. Local game data remains on your device until you reset, clear or remove it, subject to device backups. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations, and deleted when no longer needed; there is no fixed retention period stated for these messages. Google and Apple handle their own data under Google’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy and their retention practices.

For more information see the Paradox Sort Privacy Policy or visit our support page.
