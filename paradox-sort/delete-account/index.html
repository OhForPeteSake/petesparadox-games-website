How to manage your Paradox Sort data.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Paradox Sort is provided by Pete’s Paradox GAMES. The current Android and iOS versions do not offer a separate Paradox Sort player account or cloud saves. Game progress and settings are stored on your device.

Delete game data on your device

You can remove locally stored Paradox Sort data using your Android device’s app settings: open Settings → Apps → Paradox Sort → Storage and choose the option to clear app data or storage. Menu names vary by device. On iOS, delete Paradox Sort from your device to remove its locally stored app data; choosing “Offload App” keeps documents and data. Clearing storage deletes the local game data, including progress, in-game rewards, cosmetics and settings; this can’t be undone. Device or platform backups may retain or restore copies depending on your settings.

Request help or deletion without reinstalling

You can contact us without reinstalling the game. Email pete@petesparadox.games with the subject Paradox Sort data deletion request. Tell us what data you want help with and enough information for us to understand the request. Please don’t send passwords, one-time codes or payment-card details. We may ask for information needed to verify a request.

We can address information you have sent to the studio, such as support correspondence. We cannot remotely clear game progress stored only on your device or delete data held by Google or Apple on your behalf.

Google Play Games data

Google Play Games is a separate Google service. To manage or delete data in your Google Play Games profile, follow Google’s instructions for deleting Play Games data. Deleting Google Play Games data does not automatically remove information held by the game developer, and clearing local game data does not necessarily delete data held by Google.

Retention

Local game data remains on your device until you reset, clear or remove it, subject to device backups. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations, and deleted when no longer needed; there is no fixed retention period stated for these messages. Google and Apple handle their own data under Google’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy and their retention practices.

If separate Paradox Sort accounts or cloud saves are introduced later, we will update this page and provide the relevant account-deletion instructions and data-retention details before those features are enabled.

For more information see the Paradox Sort Privacy Policy or visit our support page.
