Accessibility in Paradox Sort

We want the Paradox laboratory to be welcoming to more players. Here are some of the options available in Paradox Sort. Open Settings in the game to adjust your preferences.

Text size

Use Text size to choose 100%, 125%, 150% or 200%. Larger text can make menus and instructions easier to read.

Appearance

Choose a dark or light theme, or let the game follow your device’s appearance. The dark theme uses light text on darker backgrounds.

Reduced motion

Turn on Reduced motion in Settings to reduce animations and transitions. The game also responds to the device’s reduced-motion preference for some effects.

More than colour

Sorting pieces use symbols as well as colours to help distinguish their types. Read the symbols when deciding which pieces match.

Sound, effects and haptics

In Settings you can adjust music and sound-effect volumes, mute audio, and turn visual effects or haptics on or off.

Feedback and help

Accessibility is ongoing work. If you find something difficult to use or would like to suggest an improvement, please contact pete@petesparadox.games. Let us know your device, game version and what you were trying to do. Please don’t include passwords or payment details.

Features may vary by game version and device. We continue to test and improve the experience.
