Privacy policy for Paradox Sort

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Pete’s Paradox GAMES (“we”, “us” or “our”) provides Paradox Sort. This policy describes the current Android and iOS versions based on the current game configuration. Our website has a separate privacy notice.

Information stored on your device

The game stores progress and settings on your device, including completed levels, in-game rewards and cosmetics, preferences and an unfinished game. We use this information to run the game and restore progress on that device. The game does not currently offer separate Paradox Sort player accounts or cloud saves.

Local game data remains on your device until reset, cleared or removed. Clearing app data or deleting the app can erase progress. Device and platform backups may retain or restore copies depending on your settings.

Advertising and Google services

The current Android and iOS game projects include Google Mobile Ads software and use Google’s test advertising configuration; production advertising is not enabled in the version described here. Test ad and consent requests can communicate with Google. Depending on the service and configuration, Google may process IP address, device or advertising identifiers, device and app information, interactions and diagnostics to provide and secure its services, prevent fraud, measure performance and support advertising.

The Android project also includes Google Play Games Services and Firebase software. Google Play Games may communicate with Google and may attempt to connect to a Google Play Games profile, depending on device and Google settings. The iOS project includes Google Mobile Ads; its final native service configuration may differ from Android. The game’s separate Google or Apple player sign-in is not currently available.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Play Games profile and privacy information. Where an advertising privacy-options control is available in the game, use it to review the choices offered. You can also review the privacy settings on your device and in your Google account.

Apple services and choices

On iOS, Apple provides the operating system and App Store and may process information under its own policies, including when you download the game or use device backup services. See Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can review iOS privacy, tracking and iCloud backup settings on your device. The game does not currently offer a separate Sign in with Apple account.

Purchases

Real-money purchases are not enabled in the current version described by this policy. In-game rewards and currencies are game data stored on your device. The project includes purchase software for testing; live store payments are not enabled.

When you contact us

If you contact us, we receive information you choose to send, such as your email address and details of your question, to respond and manage the request. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations, then deleted when no longer needed; no fixed retention period has been set.

Children

Paradox Sort is intended for a general audience that may include children. Parents and guardians can contact us using the support page below with questions or requests about a child’s information. Google and Apple have their own privacy practices, described in their policies linked above.

Security, retention and your choices

We use measures to protect information we handle. Third-party services describe their security and retention practices in their own privacy policies. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

You can remove local game data through Android app settings or by deleting the app on iOS; see the data deletion page for details and backup limitations. To ask about access to or deletion of information you have sent us, or to exercise rights available in your location, contact us through the support page. Google and Apple may separately process or retain information under their own policies and legal obligations.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact Pete’s Paradox GAMES through the Paradox Sort support page. We may update this policy when the game or our practices change. Before enabling production advertising, player accounts, cloud saves or real-money purchases, this policy and the game’s privacy controls must be updated to reflect those features.
