Privacy policy for Paradox Sort

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Pete’s Paradox GAMES (“we”, “us” or “our”) provides Paradox Sort. This policy describes the current Android version of the game. Our website has a separate privacy notice.

Information stored on your device

The game stores information on your device to remember your progress and settings. This can include completed levels, game progress, in-game rewards and cosmetics, preferences and an unfinished game. We use this information to run the game and restore your progress on that device.

Local game data remains on your device until it is reset, cleared or removed. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the game may erase your progress. Device or platform backups may affect what is retained or restored, depending on your settings.

Google services

The Android game includes Google Mobile Ads, Google Play Games Services and Firebase software. The current game uses Google’s test advertising configuration; production ads are not enabled in the version described here. Google Play Games may communicate with Google services and may attempt to connect to your Google Play Games profile, depending on your device and Google settings. The game’s separate player sign-in and cloud-save features are not currently available.

These services may process information such as your IP address, device or advertising identifiers, device and app information, interactions and diagnostic information. Depending on the service and configuration, Google may use this information to provide and secure its services, prevent fraud, measure performance, and support advertising. For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Play Games profile and privacy information.

Where an advertising privacy-options control is available in the game, you can use it to review the choices offered. You can also review your device and Google account privacy settings.

Purchases

Real-money purchases are not enabled in the current version described by this policy. In-game rewards and currencies shown in the game are game data stored on your device.

When you contact us

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and details of your question, so we can respond and manage the request. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations, then deleted when no longer needed.

Children

Paradox Sort is intended for a general audience that may include children. We take children’s privacy seriously. Parents and guardians can contact us using the support page below with questions or requests about a child’s information. Google services have their own privacy practices, described in Google’s policy linked above.

Security, retention and your choices

We use appropriate measures to protect information we handle. Google describes its security practices in its privacy policy; Google says the data covered by its Mobile Ads SDK disclosure is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

To remove locally stored game data, use your device’s app settings or uninstall the game, bearing in mind the backup and progress limitations above. To ask about access to or deletion of information you have sent us, or to exercise rights available in your location, contact us through the support page. Google may separately retain or process information under its own policies and legal obligations.

Contact and changes

For details on deleting local data and requesting help, see our account and data deletion page. For privacy questions or requests, contact Pete’s Paradox GAMES through the Paradox Sort support page. We may update this policy when the game or our practices change. The date at the top shows when this version was last updated. Before enabling production advertising, accounts, cloud saves or real-money purchases, this policy and the game’s privacy controls will need to be updated to reflect those features.
